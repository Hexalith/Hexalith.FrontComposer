### Aggregate views

**AG-1: Main was red at epic close, and Story 15.5's closing test changes never ran on hosted CI.** Verified. *Instance:* fix now through the existing high deferral. *Prevention:* treat gitlink moves as seal transitions, and isolate the catalog step.

- **Red at close.** At `bd189a4e`, CI 41316126833 fails `dependency-governance`. Quality 41316126396 fails `build-and-test` at "Prepare exact governance catalog objects" with `github.com/hexalith/hexalith.platform@55592ec3…: consumer source projection owner pin changed`. CI 41325767416 at HEAD fails the same way.
- **Reproduced locally.** `dependency_graph.py validate` exits 1 at HEAD and `bd189a4e`, and exits 0 at `6811bfd6`, the last main commit with green CI and Quality (41229072774 / 41229072509).
- **Cause, outside Epic 15.** `e442544c` (`fix: submodules`, 2026-10-31, no story ID) moved six root gitlinks without renewing the consumer source projection. `1e1f3fde` (a `/pushall` sync) later moved Platform from `03a099bb` to `55592ec3`. Both are tracked at `deferred-work.md:11105–11106` (high) and `:11119–11121`.
- **Effect.** Quality has skipped Gates 2b–4a ever since. The Gate 4 override-guard rows added by `c46c1fb5`, `589d63aa` and `bd189a4e` have never executed hosted. This retro ran them locally: 10 passed.
- **Amplifier.** Story 15.5's `b7bd8ea6` placed network catalog acquisition and owner-pin validation inside the sequential `build-and-test` job ahead of Gate 2b (`quality.yml:134–159`). A governance refusal therefore masks every later gate. This was raised independently by the adversarial lens (#19) and the edge-case lens: a force-push makes `github.event.before` unreachable (`quality.yml:146–147`).
- **Prevention.** Run `dependency_graph.py validate` (or reseal) before any `/pushall` or `fix: submodules` gitlink move. Move catalog acquisition into its own job so the offline gates still report. Relates to E12R-AI-1, which asked for the same gate independence for Gate 4a.

### Story-level and diff-scope findings

**R-1: `migrate --apply` rewrites files under a project-root `Generated/` directory, but refuses `src/generated/`.** Verified end to end. *Instance:* fix now (A3), small. *Prevention:* match path segments, not substrings.

**R-2: Contract diagnostics publish a provider report even after the redaction scan rejects it.** Verified as structure. *Instance:* fix now (A3). *Prevention:* make the privacy gate block publication, not just mark the run red.

- Introduced by `83544c6b`. Edge-case lens.
- Not checked: whether the provider run can contain non-synthetic credentials.
