## Acceptance verdict

**Rejected.** Both story keys are `done` and the completeness gate found no unfinished stories, but completion status cannot override failed criteria.

- Story 9.1's decision artifact and ownership/documentation criteria are supported.
- Story 9.2 AC1 is not met for lifecycle-callback terminal outcomes and is not implementable for standalone creates without a target-identity source (F9-01, F9-02).
- Story 9.2 AC2 and AC3 are not met as observable UI guarantees because indicator mutations do not invalidate an already-rendered generated grid (F9-03), and tenant/user clearing is not enforced until another `Add` (F9-04).
- The approved first-wins row contract is also incomplete for distinct message IDs targeting one row (F9-05).
- Focused tests pass, but they do not cross these composition boundaries. Live behavior verification was blocked by shared build-output locks, so there is no contrary running-system evidence.

No human override was recorded. The epic should return through normal product/spec/build/review flow using E9-AI-1 through E9-AI-6; this retrospective does not reopen or mutate the completed story records itself.
