## Detail on the gate's specific questions

- **`quality.yml` — trust input or deny-only gate; fingerprint lists?** Deny-only as a required-success precondition, and it need not be in the fingerprint lists in that role. The pass-1 sentence that makes it the home of "every Governance assertion this spine names as a pin" converts it into a trust input, and in that role it is non-conforming (`@main` actions, unauthenticated blob, absent from `select-ci`). Fix by restating it as deny-only and re-homing the pins to `prepare-candidate` and `release_contract.py` (SU2-1).
- **Builds-blob carve-out — can a lineage Builds commit smuggle a mutable action?** A literal `uses: x@main` inside a Builds blob is caught (the tool follows every `uses:` and requires 40-hex). A mutable `actions/checkout` `ref:` into `.hexalith/builds-execution` inside a Builds blob is **not** caught, nor is a caller whose `builds-execution-sha:` literal differs from its `domain-*.yml@` pin; both need a reviewed pre-authorization and are then invisible to the tool (SU2-2).
- **Ledger `actors`/`environment_protection` — evidence-not-trust wording holds?** Yes: "These records are evidence, never trust; the trust decision is the platform gate" and AD-17 keeps them out of the sealed manifest. Shape (array) and observation-time labelling are the only gaps (SU2-6).
- **`publication_started` semantics.** Conservative in the failure direction; wrong in the frozen direction because `published` keys off job conclusion while the callee succeeds when frozen (SU2-4).
- **AD-9 exact-blob fingerprints and single-use ordering.** Sound and computable before dispatch: every input (`FALLBACK_INVALIDATION_FILES` blobs at the candidate, the static inventory file, the graph digest, the policy blob, and the AD-17 digest over the authenticated CI handoff and the release caller/reusable at the candidate) is known once push CI completes. "Single-use" is a wording nit (SU2-8); the working-tree hashing in the helper is an implementation nonconformance (SU2-9a).
- **AD-16 lineage predicate — can `0a3508b3` be abused?** `0a3508b3` was in fact the live pin for ten days, so it is an ordinary superseded commit; the abuse is general: any of the 14 superseded rows is a no-policy-change downgrade target, and the predicate cannot retire a commit (SU2-3).
- **PR-gate closure recomputation.** Runs candidate code against the blobs the candidate's row names; the site that exists is the push-time `draft-evaluator` at-use check, not the described PR-gate row check. Acceptable under the Deferred acceptance once the review range is fixed (SU2-5) and the spine describes both sites (SU2-7).
