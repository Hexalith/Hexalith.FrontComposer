### RW-03 · Medium · A started publication can receive two incident dispositions

Consider a run whose authenticated marker is true, whose first product mutation fails before creating observable external state, and whose required archive is unavailable to the verifier. The classification table admits both `partial-publish-incident` and `missing-artifact`. Its precedence paragraph gives partial publication priority only with external publication evidence, then selects missing artifact. AD-15 separately requires immutable recovery for a started attempt without a complete product Release. Two producers can write different permanent dispositions for the same attempt. **Clarify the rule** for the marker-true, zero-external-effects case and its precedence over missing evidence. Pin the expected disposition and recovery obligation to a shared example.
