Architecture contract — Critical and High

Critical A1 · Candidate code runs with publication authority SEC-VAL-01 · AD-13/18

AD-18 promises that write-scoped jobs do not consume candidate code, but imposes no build/publish privilege split. The pinned reusable checks out and executes candidate restore/build, caller shell, and Semantic Release while holding GitHub write scopes, OIDC/attestation authority, NuGet credentials, and signing material.

Disposition: discuss, then require a secretless/read-only builder and a separate protected publisher/signer that treats authenticated packages as data and never executes candidate source. Amend AD-13, AD-17, and AD-18 together.

High A2 · Governed publication attempt is undefined ADV-01 · AD-15/16/18

Legacy and governed inner jobs can lead two literal verifiers to disagree about whether partial-publication inspection is mandatory.

Disposition: pin the authenticated job topology and a total governed-attempt predicate independent of one inner job name.

High A3 · Canonical JSON escape bytes are not unique ADV-02 · AD-5/9/12/14/17

\" and \u0022 are both legal readings for an accepted quote, yet hash differently across every graph, closure, fallback, provenance, and seal digest.

Disposition: bind one exact encoder contract or every escape form; add cross-language golden vectors for ambiguous characters.

High A4 · Ledger member list has no closed state machine ADV-03 / RW-03 · AD-15/18

Disposition/incident vocabularies, field types, immutable Release-attempt key, rerun policy, and REL-AI-1 projection are undefined, so incident records can diverge or be relabelled green.

Disposition: define closed types/enums, a total classifier mapping, append-only rerun semantics, and immutable attempt identity.

High A5 · Later default-branch code can decide post-release evidence SEC-VAL-02 · AD-12/15

The post-release workflow runs ambient default-branch helpers before checking out the released candidate, while those helper bytes are excluded from the trust fingerprints.

Disposition: include every executable helper in the post_release evaluator closure or use an exact pre-authorized helper tree.

Implementation — Critical and High
